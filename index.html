AI Chatbot

Conversational Engagement

Contextual legal guidance with empathetic, free-form responses

AI Chatbot handles free-form visitor messages, acknowledges specific details, provides general legal guidance, and then transitions into lead capture, helping visitors feel heard before they share contact information.

Why it fits

  • Understands complex, free-form visitor messages
  • Provides empathetic, context-aware responses
  • Offers general legal information before intake
  • Delivers better-qualified, engaged leads

Best for

  • Visitors who describe accidents, arrests, and family situations in detail
  • Firms wanting to demonstrate expertise in chat
  • Scenarios like bench warrants, probation issues, or unusual injury claims
  • A consultative tone: "I am sorry to hear…" then guide next steps

Virtual Assistant

Structured Intake Flow

Fast, predictable lead capture with mapped questions

Virtual Assistant (VA) is built for firms that want a fast, predictable intake flow. It asks mapped questions, collects contact details, and moves visitors to the next step, without answering legal questions or handling free-form narratives.

Why it fits

  • Fast lead capture with minimal friction
  • Consistent, controlled conversation paths
  • Easy to map practice areas and office locations
  • Predictable visitor experience every time

Best for

  • Firms focused on lead volume
  • Visitors who prefer buttons and short answers
  • Straightforward intake: name, case type, contact info
  • Teams that want the bot to qualify, not advise

Try these in the chat

Click Copy/Insert to add the message to the chat. Use Start Over between scenarios.

1. Give everything at once

"My name is David Smith. I was in a car accident in California on August 8, 2026. The other driver ran a red light and hit me. I broke my leg and I'm still in the hospital. My email is david@example.com and my number is 555-123-4567."

Tests: Full lead intake, case type identification, extracting multiple details, and handling contact information.

2. Start with very little information

"I got hurt at work last week and I think I need a lawyer."

Tests: Whether the bot asks the right follow-up questions instead of assuming details.

3. Add details across multiple messages

  1. Message 1 "I slipped at a grocery store."
  2. Message 2 "It happened in California on August 8."
  3. Message 3 "There was liquid on the floor and no warning sign."
  4. Message 4 "I broke my ankle and had surgery."

Tests: Conversation memory and whether the bot builds the case as information is provided.

4. Test a complex Workers' Comp case

"I injured my back lifting equipment at work in California on August 8. I told my supervisor that day but kept working. A week later the pain got worse and I had to stop working. My employer says I should have reported it sooner."

Tests: Workers' Comp identification, timeline extraction, and handling a more complicated scenario.
