AI Chatbot

Conversational Engagement

Contextual legal guidance with empathetic, free-form responses

AI Chatbot handles free-form visitor messages, acknowledges specific details, provides general legal guidance, and then transitions into lead capture, helping visitors feel heard before they share contact information.

Why it fits

  • Understands complex, free-form visitor messages
  • Provides empathetic, context-aware responses
  • Offers general legal information before intake
  • Delivers better-qualified, engaged leads

Best for

  • Visitors who describe accidents, arrests, and family situations in detail
  • Firms wanting to demonstrate expertise in chat
  • Scenarios like bench warrants, probation issues, or unusual injury claims
  • A consultative tone: "I am sorry to hear…" then guide next steps

Virtual Assistant

Structured Intake Flow

Fast, predictable lead capture with mapped questions

Virtual Assistant (VA) is built for firms that want a fast, predictable intake flow. It asks mapped questions, collects contact details, and moves visitors to the next step, without answering legal questions or handling free-form narratives.

Why it fits

  • Fast lead capture with minimal friction
  • Consistent, controlled conversation paths
  • Easy to map practice areas and office locations
  • Predictable visitor experience every time

Best for

  • Firms focused on lead volume
  • Visitors who prefer buttons and short answers
  • Straightforward intake: name, case type, contact info
  • Teams that want the bot to qualify, not advise
